# 01 — Research and prior art

Gathered 2026-11-14 by web research. Most design-side sources are wikis, press, and forum posts; few are official. Items marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed from a source and should not be relied on.

## 1. Use-based skill systems

- **Mabinogi.** Each rank has a list of training conditions that together supply 100 skill-XP; AP is then spent to rank up, and AP cost rises with rank. Enemy ratings ("Strong", "Awful", "Boss") depend on the enemy's combat power relative to yours, so the same condition changes as you grow; one wiki example goes from 400 kills at Boss rating to 3,000 at Awful. Players describe the result as "use a skill a specific subtle way almost 1000 times". [Skill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skill), [Rank](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rank), [CP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/CP), [forum](https://forums.mabinogi.nexon.net/discussion/comment/138388). A specific "fail X times" requirement is UNVERIFIED.
- **Ultima Online.** A 700-point total skill cap (720 from 2013). Players trapped monsters to macro on and exploited per-map-chunk random seeds ("8x8"). [Skill cap](https://uoguide.com/Skill_Cap).
- **Raph Koster, "Use-based systems" (2006).** Fixes: reward results instead of repetitions, scale gains to difficulty overcome, and treat skills as tools for other goals. [Essay](https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=592).
- **Elder Scrolls.** Oblivion Remastered still allows AFK Sneak and jumping for Acrobatics. [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/the-elder-scrolls/oblivion-remastered-leveling-explained-skills-list).
- **Project Gorgon.** Developers required actual use after players levelled one skill pair and raced through the rest. [Forum](https://forum.projectgorgon.com/showthread.php?p=1379).

**Used in:** [02](02-progression.md) — Challenge factor (difficulty), Freshness (per-context caps), Provenance (results-based), no second currency.

## 2. Housing

- **Final Fantasy XIV.** 9,000 plots per world plus apartments; a lottery on a nine-day cycle; auto-demolition after 45 days of absence (paused at times); furnishing caps from 150 (apartment) to 600 indoors (large). [Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing), [critique](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/12871-final-fantasy-xivs-housing-situation-is-still-a-depressing-mess-and-it-deserves-better).
- **EverQuest II.** Every character gets a house; free placement; repeatedly named best MMO housing. [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2019/12/15/massively-ops-2019-awards-best-mmo-housing/).
- **Star Wars Galaxies.** Open-world houses of lapsed accounts became ghost towns until a demolition programme. [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2017/10/19/massively-overthinking-is-open-world-housing-really-a-failed-mmorg-experiment/).
- **Palia.** Instanced plot, up to 3,000 items; Home Tours let others visit a copy while the owner is offline. [Plot](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Housing_Plot), [Tours](https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Home_Tours).
- **RuneScape.** Instanced house reached from many towns; 2026 rework allows 25 rooms and 500 furniture. [Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Player-owned_house).

Trade-off: instancing removes scarcity and isolates homes; open-world placement creates presence and needs demolition rules.

**Used in:** [09](09-housing.md) — guaranteed instanced homesteads, hamlets for presence, offline visiting, no demolition.

## 3. Dress-up and inclusive character creation

- **Love Nikki.** Close to ten thousand items; each has style tags that stages score against. [Tampere game lab](https://www.tuni.fi/playlab/love-nikki-dress-up-queen-where-bookshelves-count-as-accessories/). Slots seen in fan data include hair, dress, coat, top, bottom, hosiery, shoes, and many accessory positions. [Fan wiki](https://lovenikki.fandom.com/wiki/Target_Suits). The exact slot count and attribute pairs are UNVERIFIED.
- **Infinity Nikki 1.5.** Reviews fell to "mixed" over a paywalled dye system and outfit pricing. [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/infinity-nikki-is-facing-review-bombing-on-steam-after-turning-in/z37dec).
- **The Sims 4 (2016).** Opened 700+ items to every gender; body frame, voice, and walk chosen independently. [EA](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/news/the-sims-4-adding-more-choices).

**Used in:** [08](08-wardrobe.md).

## 4. Grouping

- Role-queue waits depend on role mix. [Blizzard](https://us.support.blizzard.com/en/article/323640).
- Final Fantasy XI added Trusts (NPC party members); Guild Wars added heroes. [Trusts](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Category:Trust), [Heroes](https://wiki.guildwars.com/wiki/Hero).
- EverQuest II mentoring; Guild Wars 2 automatic down-scaling. [EQ2](https://www.everquest2.com/news/imported-eq2-enus-396), [GW2](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Sidekick).
- "Alone Together?" (Ducheneaut et al., CHI 2006): WoW players mostly play beside each other, not with each other. [Paper](https://dx.doi.org/10.1145/1124772.1124834).

**Used in:** [04](04-combat-and-party.md) — parties of four, flex roles, Echoes capped below a human, sync-down with a mentor reward.

## 5. General pitfalls

- Mudflation and the sideways-advancement fix. [Boing Boing](https://boingboing.net/2007/01/19/mudflation-inflation.html).
- Dailies as chores. [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2019/10/31/massively-overthinking-the-problem-with-mmo-dailies/).
- Riot (GDC 2025): fewer dailies cost short-term retention and pay off long-term. [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/strategy/the-worlds-largest-pc-strategy-game-has-a-novel-idea-what-if-it-was-ok-for-people-to-leave).

## 6. Craft minigames

- **Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 smithing.** Heat judged by glow colour; hold for heavy strikes; cover the whole workpiece; keep a rhythm; running out of heat sends you back to reheat. Quality = play + character level + buffs. [Guide](https://game-checklists.com/kcd2/guides/blacksmithing-guide/).
- **Bladesong (2026).** Hammer blows deform the blade; the customer's spec is weight, balance, and shape. [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2305640).
- **Dressmaker (2026).** Measure, sketch, lay pattern pieces on-grain, cut, steer each seam. An off-grain cut caps quality. Has a sewing-assist option. [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/sim/dressmaker-is-a-serious-seamstress-sim-with-a-lot-of-love-stitched-into-the-finer-details).
- **Potion Craft.** Ingredients move a marker along fixed paths on a hidden map. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/potion-craft-has-finally-given-me-a-crafting-system-i-dont-hate).
- **Atelier Sophie.** Shape-packing on a grid. [Siliconera](https://siliconera.com/atelier-sophie-has-a-new-puzzle-like-synthesis-system).
- **Final Fantasy XIV.** Deterministic progress/quality/durability rotations are solved by community tools; Expert recipes add random conditions. [Expert recipes](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Expert_Recipes).
- **EverQuest II.** Reactive events could be ignored at the cost of time only, so players ignored them. [Forum archive](https://archive.eq2wire.com/showthread.php?p=5071222).
- **Mabinogi tailoring.** The target cursor shrinks as character skill rises. [Wiki](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tailor).
- **Fantasy Life i.** Character stat outweighs input: a warning. [Guide](https://www.gamerguides.com/fantasy-life-i-the-girl-who-steals-time/guide/getting-started/introduction/crafting-system-explained).
- **While the Iron's Hot.** One-at-a-time crafting with no batching was called tedious. [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/review-while-the-irons-hot-puts-you-to-work-as-a-blacksmith/).

Synthesis: minigames stay engaging when each run has different state, materials change the puzzle, and character skill widens tolerance instead of replacing input. They get macroed when state is deterministic, when the reactive layer can be ignored, when stats overpower content, and when there is no batching.

## 7. Quality, decay, and crafter relevance

- **Star Wars Galaxies.** Each resource spawn is unique, has its own stats, lasts roughly 6–21 days, and never returns. Input quality caps output. Factories produce at base quality with no experimentation. Repair can lower maximum condition. [Resources](https://swgr.org/wiki/crafting_resources/), [Crafting](https://swgr.org/wiki/crafting).
- **Wurm Online.** Output quality comes from material, skill, and tool quality. [Wurmpedia](https://wurmpedia.com/index.php/Quality_Level).
- **EVE Online.** Destruction keeps industry alive. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/mmo/eve-online-players-destroyed-1-142-260-784-813-930-isk-worth-of-ships-in-2025-which-according-to-my-caveman-level-maths-amounts-to-over-usd6-million-lost-to-the-void).
- **Koster on SWG.** At peak, half of players ran a shop. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/star-wars-galaxies-devs-discuss-why-it-died).

## 8. Automation

- **SWG harvesters** need maintenance and power; unpaid ones stall and decay. [Guide](https://swgaide.com/swgaide_help_resources_harvesting_en.html).
- **EVE moon mining.** Passive siphons were replaced by fleet-mined chunks; the later automated drill is deliberately slower. [EVE University](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Moon_mining).
- **Foxhole.** Facility upkeep rises with server load. [Wiki](https://foxhole.wiki.gg/wiki/Update_1.52).
- **Albion laborers.** Yield depends on journals filled by playing. [Guide](https://heaven-guardian.com/albion-online-laborers-guide/).
- **Black Desert.** Worker networks and AFK activities are criticised as opaque AFK play. [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/black-desert-afk-guide,265238/).
- **Brighter Shores.** Official offline skilling at reduced rate; the stated reason is that people bot out of frustration with grind. [MMOs.com](https://mmos.com/news/brighter-shores-afk-skilling-system).

## 9. Anti-bot for skill checks

- Server-side behaviour profiling (Jagex BotWatch), including a 2012 false-ban incident. [OSRS wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:DevBlog:_BotWatch_Update_in_Old_School_RuneScape).
- Replay analysis in osu! (timing variance, frame times, similarity). Indicators need human review. [Circleguard](https://github.com/circleguard/circleguard).
- Server re-simulation of input traces from a server-held seed. [Photon Quantum](https://doc.photonengine.com/quantum/v2/manual/cheat-protection).
- Bots add jitter, so combine signals. [arXiv 1509.02458](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.02458).

## 10. Server technology

- **Languages.** Discord moved a hot service from Go to Rust over garbage-collection latency spikes ([blog](https://discord.com/blog/why-discord-is-switching-from-go-to-rust)). Albion's server is C# on Photon ([slides](https://www.slideshare.net/davidsalz54/albion-online-software-architecture-of-an-mmo-talk-at-quo-vadis-2016-berlin)). BitCraft runs on SpacetimeDB in Rust ([repo](https://github.com/clockworklabs/SpacetimeDB)). CISA/NSA guidance (June 2025) attributes 66–75% of CVEs to memory-safety bugs ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/27/cisa_nsa_call_formemory_safe_languages/)).
- **SpacetimeDB.** Business Source License 1.1 (one production instance; converts to AGPLv3 in 2031) ([licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/clockworklabs/SpacetimeDB/master/LICENSE.txt)). No built-in client prediction ([request](https://spacetimedb.com/features/requests/17)). BitCraft's launch had stalls from unoptimised queries ([post](https://bitcraftonline.com/news/early-access-day-2-update-the-server-saga)).
- **Albion's architecture (2016).** About 600 zones, each simulated on one thread; hand-off through the database; up to 300 players per zone; roughly 15,000 concurrent players on 8 ten-core servers (about 190 per core, derived). Interest management on a 10 m grid.
- **SpatialOS.** Several games built on dynamic spatial partitioning were cancelled, citing cost. [PCGamesInsider](https://www.pcgamesinsider.biz/news/69671/bossa-doesnt-blame-improbable-for-worlds-adrifts-cancellation/).
- **EVE time dilation.** Slow the clock instead of dropping work. [CCP](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/introducing-time-dilation-tidi).
- **Rust ecosystem (Nov 2026).** quinn 0.11.x — pin 0.11.14 or later for CVE-2026-31812. wtransport 0.7.x describes itself as not completely production-ready. Bevy ships breaking releases every three to four months. WebTransport is reported in Safari 26.4 and Baseline ([WebRTC.ventures](https://webrtc.ventures/2026/04/webtransport-is-now-baseline-what-it-means-for-real-time-media/)); test before relying on it. Exact crate versions must be re-checked before pinning.
- **Dupes.** New World (2021): sending gold then disconnecting during a server transfer rolled the sender back, so the gold existed twice. [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/new-world/gold-duping-bug).
- **DDoS.** AWS GameLift player gateway and DDoS protection (no added charge with GameLift) ([AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/gameliftservers/latest/developerguide/ddos-protection-intro.html)); OVHcloud game servers with UDP-tuned protection from roughly €65–140 a month ([OVH](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/bare-metal/game/)); Cloudflare Spectrum for UDP needs an Enterprise plan.
- No published tick rates or per-core capacities were found for comparable games. Capacity must be load-tested.

## 11. Client technology

- **PixiJS 8** (8.20, August 2026): WebGL default, WebGPU opt-in; KTX2/Basis loaders. [Docs](https://pixijs.com/8.x/guides/components/assets/compressed-textures).
- **Godot 4.7** (June 2026): Compatibility renderer runs on 2012 integrated graphics; web export is WebGL2. Rust bindings (gdext 0.5) on the web are experimental: nightly Rust, two wasm builds, cross-origin isolation for threads. [gdext book](https://godot-rust.github.io/book/toolchain/export-web.html).
- **Bevy 0.19/0.20**: shares the most Rust; no editor; UI is the weakest of the three.
- **Layered characters.** Bake each character's layers into one atlas page ([Spine forum](https://esotericsoftware.com/forum/d/28182-working-with-big-atlasesa-lot-of-skins-in-godot)). Liberated Pixel Cup keeps head and hand positions identical across adult bodies so hats, hair, and weapons fit all ([LPC](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-character-bases)). Soulbound (2026) describes frame-by-frame layered gear as "a lot of work" ([post](https://soulbound.game/news/how-we-built-a-pixel-art-character-and-cosmetic-system/)).
- **Player caps.** Guild Wars 2 shows players beyond a limit as nameplates. [Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Culling).
- **Testing.** Headless Chromium can render black WebGL canvases under software rendering; pin one backend and generate screenshot baselines in CI only. [Microlink](https://microlink.io/blog/webgl-without-a-gpu).
